# Research

For issue #14. Draft written by Claude from general knowledge of these apps and the features in [[Interview Loop Backlog]]. Check it against what you found and fix anything that's wrong.

## Apps looked at
| App | What it does well | What's missing |
|---|---|---|
| LeetCode | Huge question bank, company-tagged questions, code editor | No interviewer, no full loop, no behavioral or system design practice |
| Pramp | Free live mock interviews with other students | Depends on a peer showing up; quality varies; no AI |
| interviewing.io | Mock interviews with real engineers from big tech | Expensive; you have to book sessions |
| Exponent | Courses and videos on system design and behavioral | Mostly watching, not much live practice |
| Final Round AI | AI interviewer and feedback | Focused on single interviews, not a full multi-round loop |

## The gap
No app runs a **full interview loop** (several rounds back to back, like a real Amazon or Google onsite) with **one AI voice interviewer** guiding you through it, plus detailed feedback on every round at the end. That's what Interview Loop does.

## Features users need
**Must have (version 1)**
- Start a loop: pick a company mode (Amazon first)
- 4 round types: coding, low-level design, and behavioral (see below)
- Code editor, whiteboard for design, and a timer
- Live AI voice interviewer that talks to you and asks follow-ups, with live captions
- All feedback on one results page at the end of the loop

**Later**
- More company modes (Google, Meta, etc.)
- Big system design rounds (for senior roles)
- Bigger question bank
- Progress tracking and sign-in

## What each round tests (Amazon, new grad)
Based on candidate reports online (Glassdoor, Reddit, prep blogs), not official Amazon info. Exact rounds change by team and year.

| Round | What it tests |
|---|---|
| Coding (usually 2 rounds) | Data structures and algorithms, LeetCode-style |
| Low-level design | Designing classes for a system (object-oriented), e.g. a parking lot |
| Behavioral / Bar Raiser | Leadership Principles stories, answered with STAR |

- Leadership Principle questions show up in almost every round, not just behavioral.
- The Bar Raiser is someone from outside the team who helps make the final decision.
- New grads usually don't get big "system design" (that's for more senior roles).
- Meta new grad: usually 2 coding + 1 behavioral. Google new grad: mostly coding.

Sources: simplify.jobs/blog/amazon-swe-interview-new-grads, simplify.jobs/blog/meta-new-grad-swe-interview, datainterview.com/blog/amazon-software-engineer-interview
