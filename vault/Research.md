# Research

For issue #14. Draft written by Claude from general knowledge of these apps and the features in [[Interview Loop Backlog]]. Check it against what you found and fix anything that's wrong.

## Apps looked at
| App | What it does well | What's missing |
|---|---|---|
| LeetCode | Huge question bank, company-tagged questions, code editor | No interviewer, no full loop, no behavioral or system design practice |
| Pramp | Free live mock interviews with other students | Depends on a peer showing up; quality varies; no AI |
| interviewing.io | Mock interviews with real engineers from big tech | Expensive; you have to book sessions |
| Exponent | Courses and videos on system design and behavioral | Mostly watching, not much live practice |
| Final Round AI | AI interviewer and feedback | Focused on single interviews, not a full multi-round loop |

## The gap
No app runs a **full interview loop** (several rounds back to back, like a real Amazon or Google onsite) with **one AI voice interviewer** guiding you through it, plus feedback after each round. That's what Interview Loop does.

## Features users need
**Must have (version 1)**
- Start a loop: pick a company mode (Amazon first)
- Coding round with a question, a code editor, and a timer
- AI interviewer that asks the question and follow-ups (text first, voice later)
- Feedback after each round

**Later**
- AI voice interviewer
- More company modes (Google, Meta, etc.)
- System design and behavioral rounds
- Bigger question bank
- Progress tracking and sign-in
