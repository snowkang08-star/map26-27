# Interview Loop (map 26-27)

## What it is
An app that helps students prepare for tech job interviews, mainly computer science. It simulates real interviews like the ones at Amazon and other FAANG companies.

## Goals
- Practice coding interview questions like the ones big tech companies ask
- Simulate a full "interview loop" (several rounds in a row, like the real thing)
- Have an AI voice interviewer that talks to the user through the whole loop
- Give feedback after each round
- Track progress over time
